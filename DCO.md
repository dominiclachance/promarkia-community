# Developer Certificate of Origin

Contributions are accepted under the same MIT license as the repository and must certify the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/). Add this line to every
commit, using your real name and email address:

`Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>`

By adding that line, you certify that you have the right to submit the contribution under the
project license and agree that the contribution and sign-off record are public. The repository's
pull-request check rejects commits without a valid sign-off line.
